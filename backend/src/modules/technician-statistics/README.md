# STT123 technician statistics

Admin-only `GET /api/admin/technician-statistics` is read-only, no-store and audited using aggregate metadata only. The endpoint uses one repeatable-read, read-only transaction. Inclusive UTC+7 periods default to 30 days and have a maximum of 366 days; up to 90 days use daily buckets, longer ranges use monthly buckets. Literal search (max 150 characters), page and limit (10/20/50) affect only the current locked-account table.

## Snapshot versus period

- The current population is every technician profile joined to its user, including pending/rejected and inactive profiles. Technicians retain their CUSTOMER role and can also be customers; no role-exclusive classification is applied. Active/inactive/locked follow the existing Admin technician list: LOCKED account first, ACTIVE account plus active profile second, otherwise INACTIVE. Verification is a separate, overlapping dimension.
- Current coverage uses all distinct, nonblank city/ward pairs in `technician_areas`. Each technician is counted once per area. The same technician may serve several areas, so coverage counts overlap and percentages use total technician population rather than sum-to-100 shares. Profiles without any complete city/ward pair are unassigned.
- Locked accounts are current `users.status='LOCKED'`. The table shows the actual technician-profile registration date, never `updated_at` as a supposed lock date. Historical locks, reinstatements, ONLINE trends, targets and SLA thresholds are unavailable and are not synthesized.

## Activity and quality

- A request is a distinct technician/booking pair. When duplicate assignment rows exist, its most recently requested row (ID descending as tie breaker) is chosen before date filtering. Only SELECTED, ACCEPTED, DECLINED, EXPIRED, COMPLETED and CANCELLED are sent requests. RECOMMENDED and NOT_SELECTED are not counted. The selected period is the assignment request date; subsequent outcomes are its current state, not activity events that necessarily occurred inside that period.
- Accepted means current ACCEPTED/COMPLETED, or an ACCEPTED booking-history entry made by that technician at or after this request. SELECTED is not acceptance. Completed requires both the assignment and booking to be COMPLETED. Completion percentage divides completed pairs by all sent pairs in the same request cohort. One booking sent to different technicians contributes to each corresponding request pair, unlike a platform-wide distinct booking count.
- A technician cancellation requires both the assignment and booking to be CANCELLED and the recorded booking cancellation actor to be that technician's user. A technician decline also cancels the booking in the job-request flow, but its DECLINED assignment is counted only as a decline, not cancellation of accepted work. A CANCELLED assignment alone is insufficient: the customer cancellation flow also marks assignments CANCELLED. Other cancelled assignments are kept separately. Technician declines use DECLINED, not customer quotation rejection. Accepted is a historical stage and can overlap later completion/cancellation; these fields are not additive status partitions.
- Reviews are aggregated independently from assignments: only PUBLISHED rows created in the selected period. The average is total stars divided by actual review count, not an average of cached technician averages. No reviews means unavailable, not a zero-star rating. Review trend buckets use review creation dates; request trend buckets use request dates.

## Entitlement and issues

- Income is SUCCESS payments with a non-null settlement date in the selected period, owned by a current technician profile. The immutable `technician_earning` snapshot is entitlement including parts reimbursement, not profit, gross customer payment, commission, wallet credit, deposit or withdrawal. No settled payment is multiplied by assignment, review, complaint or area joins. Completed income jobs are distinct bookings whose current status is COMPLETED among those settled payments.
- Known amounts are exact decimal strings. Any missing eligible entitlement makes total income, average income and the top-technician ranking unavailable; known partial totals and per-bucket missing counts remain available separately. A zero-payment period has known total 0, unavailable average and no winner. Average denominator is the number of distinct technicians with an eligible settled payment, including zero entitlements. Equal exact income ranks by technician ID.
- Complaints are rows with an explicit technician link, created in the selected period. OPEN/IN_PROGRESS are pending; RESOLVED, REJECTED and CLOSED retain separate current status counts. A resolved-series point is the current resolved state of complaints created in that bucket, not a historical count of resolution actions.
- Penalties are records applied in the selected period, including later expired/revoked records because they were applied in that period. They are not equivalent to account locks. Total issues is complaint records plus penalty records, not deduplicated incidents; one incident may produce both. Affected technicians are the union across both sources.

All financial, assignment, review, complaint and coverage sources are aggregated independently before composing the response. The feature performs no schema migration, fixture insertion, status update, financial settlement or other business write.
